# Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
The **Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze business sales, revenue, profit and financial performance across different countries, products and market segments.

The dashboard provides key business insights through interactive slicers, KPI cards and visualizations, helping users understand sales trends, identify top-performing products and evaluate profitability.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, revenue and profit performance.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends over time.
- Compare sales and profit across different countries.
- Identify top-performing products based on revenue.
- Understand sales distribution across market segments.
- Evaluate the impact of different discount bands on sales and profit.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Analysis and Visualization

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:
- Year
- Country
- Product
- Segment

These filters allow users to explore business performance based on selected categories.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Units Sold:** Tracks the total quantity of products sold.
- **Total Sales:** Displays overall sales value.
- **Profit Margin:** Measures profitability as a percentage of sales.
- **Total Profit:** Shows the overall profit generated.

### 3. Visualizations

| Visualization | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Monthly Sales, Revenue & Profit Trend | Analyzes financial performance over time. |
| Sales & Profit by Country | Compares business performance across countries. |
| Sales Share by Market Segment | Shows sales distribution across customer segments. |
| Top Products by Revenue | Identifies products generating the highest revenue. |
| Discount Band Impact | Evaluates the relationship between discount levels, sales and profit. |

## Key Insights
- Government and Small Business segments contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Paseo is the leading product in terms of revenue.
- Canada and the United States of America show strong sales performance.
- Monthly sales and profit fluctuate over time, highlighting variations in business performance.
- Different discount bands show variations in sales and profit generation.

*Note: Insights are based on the dashboard visualizations and should be validated against the underlying dataset.*
